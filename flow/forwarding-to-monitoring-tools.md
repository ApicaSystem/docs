# Forwarding to Target Destinations

Data can be filtered and forwarded to many different monitoring tools, resource management tools, and custom endpoints. Common target destinations for forwarding include:

* [Splunk](https://docs.apica.io/~/revisions/aKlJTfkbFwMOe3RTTbTr/flow/list-of-forwarders/splunk-forwarding)
* [Datadog](https://docs.apica.io/~/revisions/o9hazJIbeR9dFxVZtwXj/flow/list-of-forwarders/datadog-forwarding)
* [New Relic](https://docs.apica.io/~/revisions/o9hazJIbeR9dFxVZtwXj/flow/list-of-forwarders/new-relic-forwarding)
* [Dynatrace](https://docs.apica.io/~/revisions/o9hazJIbeR9dFxVZtwXj/flow/list-of-forwarders/dynatrace-forwarding)
* [Elasticsearch](https://docs.apica.io/~/revisions/py1Rsp0gcBp6CQLWLeQp/flow/list-of-forwarders/elasticsearch-forwarding)
* [Coralogix](https://docs.apica.io/~/revisions/py1Rsp0gcBp6CQLWLeQp/flow/list-of-forwarders/coralogix-forwarding)
* [Azure Log Analytics](https://docs.apica.io/~/revisions/py1Rsp0gcBp6CQLWLeQp/flow/list-of-forwarders/azure-log-analytics-forwarding)
* [OpenTelemetry](https://docs.apica.io/~/revisions/py1Rsp0gcBp6CQLWLeQp/flow/opentelemetry-forwarding)
* [JS Code](https://docs.apica.io/~/revisions/py1Rsp0gcBp6CQLWLeQp/flow/list-of-forwarders/js-code-forwarding)
* &#x20;... and others

Here is a [list of available forwarders](list-of-forwarders.md) (this list is always growing). You can contact support@apica.io to obtain details of additional forwarders if you do not see your specific tool/technology listed.
